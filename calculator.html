Every Vegetable Is Different

Seeding rates for vegetables vary hugely by type — tomato, pepper, okra, onion and garden eggs are not planted the same way. Rather than guess, chat with us and we'll recommend the right seed quantity for your specific crop and farm size.

How we calculate this

We use typical certified-seed seeding rates per hectare, then scale to your farm size:

  • Maize: ~20–25 kg/ha (22 kg/ha typical)
  • Rice (direct seeded): ~40–60 kg/ha (50 kg/ha typical)
  • Soybean: ~60–80 kg/ha (70 kg/ha typical)

These are general agronomic guide rates, not Qualiseed-specific specifications. Your ideal rate can shift with variety, spacing, soil fertility and planting method — use the "Advanced" option above to plug in your own rate if you have one, or ask our team for a tailored recommendation.
